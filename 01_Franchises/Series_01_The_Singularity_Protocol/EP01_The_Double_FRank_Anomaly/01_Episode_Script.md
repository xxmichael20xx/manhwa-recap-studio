### ACT 1: THE EVALUATION CEREMONY & THE BLIND CRYSTAL

[IMG_001]
In the Imperial Hunter Academy of Valerius, human worth is quantified by a single unforgiving metric: the raw destructive volume of your awakened mana core.

[IMG_002]
Those who conjure towering firestorms or shatter granite with lightning are hailed as sovereign national protectors, immediately granted military pensions and noble rank.

[IMG_003]
Meanwhile, utility wielders with non-destructive affinities are quietly discarded into the hazardous scavenger divisions, doomed to haul dimensional ballast until their contracts expire.

[IMG_004]
The Grand Awakening Arena buzzed with feverish anticipation as three thousand spectators filled the tiered obsidian stands under blazing imperial spotlights.

[IMG_005]
At the center of the stadium, Prince Valen Sol-Aegis stepped toward the colossal Resonance Pillar with theatrical composure.

[IMG_006]
The nineteen-year-old royal heir radiated supreme condescension, his golden-blonde hair and pristine white-and-gold uniform pristine beneath the glare of floating press drones.

[IMG_007]
When Valen's gloved hand touched the obsidian crystal, a deafening roar shook the colosseum as a blinding fifty-metre pillar of solar plasma erupted toward the sky.

[IMG_008]
The automated diagnostic display locked in with imperial fanfare: S-Rank Solar Flare, initial mana saturation index calibrated at five thousand units.

[IMG_009]
House Sol-Aegis proctors erupted in standing ovation, their family secure in another multi-million-credit state defense subsidy before midday concluded.

[IMG_010]
Valen sneered down at the lower tiers, adjusting his crimson silk capelet as though the surrounding students were mere background noise.

[IMG_011]
Then, Head Proctor Keith Morgan called the next name from the registry: Caelen Vance.

[IMG_012]
Unlike the aristocrats draped in enchanted silks, Caelen stepped onto the stage wearing an unadorned charcoal cadet tunic, dark hair falling messily across sharp, observant violet eyes.

[IMG_013]
For three grueling years, Caelen had studied dimensional physics in the academy archives, knowing true power lay not in flashy fireworks, but in the fundamental constants of spacetime.

[IMG_014]
Caelen calmly placed his bare palm against the frozen surface of the Resonance Pillar.

[IMG_015]
The massive monolith hummed with a deep, subterranean acoustic vibration, yet no flames roared and no lightning split the air.

[IMG_016]
To the impatient examiners in the observation booth, the ancient testing pillar appeared entirely dormant, its elemental needles flatlining at zero.

[IMG_017]
The automated registrar chimed with an emotionless mechanical voice: Double F-Rank evaluation recorded.

[IMG_018]
Skill One: Micro-Spatial Fold, range restricted to a pathetic one millimetre. Skill Two: Localized Point Gravity Shift, limited strictly to physical touch.

[IMG_019]
Mocking laughter swept through the noble stands as examiners stamped a crimson rejection seal across Caelen's dossier: Assigned to Scavenger Unit.

[IMG_020]
High in the proctor box, Head Examiner Keith Morgan watched Caelen step down without a flicker of disappointment in his posture.

[IMG_021]
The battle-scarred veteran narrowed his eyes, his enchanted cybernetic prosthetic arm humming as he zoomed his visual sensors onto the testing pedestal.

[IMG_022]
Beneath Caelen's boots, the reinforced granite slab hadn't melted from heat—it had been compressed into solid industrial diamond under fifty atmospheres of silent gravitational force.

[IMG_023]
Caelen quietly walked toward the Class F benches, tightening the straps of his fingerless grip gloves with an unbothered smirk.

[IMG_024]
The academy's primitive testing arrays were built solely for crude chemical combustion; they could never comprehend that he held absolute administrative authority over Space and Gravity.

### ACT 2: THE OBSIDIAN HOLLOWS & THE ARISTOCRAT'S BETRAYAL

[IMG_025]
The annual Academy Survival Trial commenced three hundred metres beneath the capital within the perilous subterranean labyrinth known as the Obsidian Hollows.

[IMG_026]
The protocol was ruthless: Class A elites pushed the vanguard to farm glory and monster cores, while Class F porters marched in the rear, hauling hundreds of kilograms of extraction ballast.

[IMG_027]
Caelen marched in the rear flank alongside Lyra Fenn, a timid first-year alchemist demoted to Class F after failing her offensive casting exam.

[IMG_028]
Lyra stumbled beneath the heavy weight of her alchemical haul-pack, her round brass goggles sliding down her nose as she surveyed the jagged cavern ceiling.

[IMG_029]
"Keep close to the left wall, Lyra," Caelen instructed quietly, observing the damp mineral formations and acoustic reverberations of the trench.

[IMG_030]
"The corridor here naturally funnels atmospheric pressure; if the vanguard causes a breach, this center path will transform into a lethal kill-zone."

[IMG_031]
Ahead of them, Prince Valen commanded the forward squad with reckless vanity, incinerating low-tier Shadow Crawlers with wide-arc solar beams.

[IMG_032]
Valen incinerated harmless fodder to show off for the floating camera drones, utterly disregarding the fragile structural integrity of the surrounding cavern arches.

[IMG_033]
Caelen counted Valen's mana expenditure: thirty percent wasted on aesthetic flourishes while basic subterranean protocol dictated defensive reserve.

[IMG_034]
Without warning, the stone floor convulsed violently under a sickening frequency of spatial distortion.

[IMG_035]
A jagged crimson rift tore open across the main tunnel, corrupting the dungeon mana field and swallowing the low-tier monster spawns in an instant.

[IMG_036]
From the dimensional tear emerged the horrifying silhouette of an unscheduled Calamity Mutation: the Void-Carapace Goliath.

[IMG_037]
Standing over four metres tall, the chitinous titan possessed obsidian armor plating vibrating with anti-magic dimensional frequencies and dual razor scythe forelimbs.

[IMG_038]
Panic instantly shattered the Class A vanguard as their elemental barriers flickered out against the Goliath's anti-magic aura.

[IMG_039]
Valen shrieked in desperation, charging his signature S-Rank Solar Lance and hurling a massive concentrated plasma javelin at the monster's skull.

[IMG_040]
The radiant lance struck the Goliath's cranium with blinding brilliance, but dissipated harmlessly into thermal heat across its void-plated exoskeleton. Zero damage inflicted.

[IMG_041]
The Goliath didn't even flinch; it lunged forward with hydraulic fury, snapping the vanguard's reinforced mithril shields like dry kindling.

[IMG_042]
Faced with genuine mortality, Prince Valen's aristocratic arrogance evaporated into cowardly panic as he broke formation and bolted toward the extraction lift.

[IMG_043]
"Seal the quarantine gate!" Valen screamed at the mechanical operator, clawing his way into the reinforced elevator cage.

[IMG_044]
To ensure his own escape, Valen forcefully pulled the emergency iron lever, dropping a three-ton spiked iron portcullis behind him.

[IMG_045]
The heavy iron teeth slammed into the stone foundation with an ear-splitting crash, locking Caelen, Lyra, and the Class F squad inside the dark cavern.

[IMG_046]
Trapped behind the bars, the porter students fell to their knees in despair as the Goliath turned its glowing purple optical clusters toward them.

[IMG_047]
"Valen abandoned us... we're dead!" a student wailed, dropping his iron pickaxe against the dust.

[IMG_048]
In the pitch-black cavern illuminated only by the monster's violet rift fissures, Caelen stepped forward, dropping his extraction satchel without a hint of fear.

### ACT 3: THE PHYSICS MASSACRE & ZERO DAMAGE THRESHOLD

[IMG_049]
The Goliath emitted an ear-splitting screech, its muscular segmented legs propelling its massive bulk across the cavern floor at terrifying speed.

[IMG_050]
Its dual razor scythes whistled through the air, descending in a lethal downward execution arc aimed directly at Caelen's skull.

[IMG_051]
Lyra shrieked, shutting her eyes and covering her face in terror as the heavy obsidian blade made contact.

[IMG_052]
Yet there was no sound of tearing flesh and no spray of blood.

[IMG_053]
At precisely one millimetre from Caelen's chest, the Goliath's lethal scythe ground to a dead, absolute halt against an invisible barrier.

[IMG_054]
Caelen's Micro-Spatial Fold had curved the local geometry of space, redirecting thousands of kilograms of kinetic momentum into an adjacent dimensional void.

[IMG_055]
To the Calamity monster, it was as if its strike had collided with the unyielding mass of an entire planetary core. Zero damage penetration.

[IMG_056]
Caelen looked up into the beast's glowing purple eyes, his own irises swirling with deep amethyst gravitational rings.

[IMG_057]
"My turn," Caelen murmured in a chillingly calm monotone.

[IMG_058]
He stepped into the monster's reach and pressed his flat palm against the center of its unbreakable chitinous chest plate.

[IMG_059]
Activating Skill Two: Point Gravitational Density Shift, Caelen concentrated fifty times planetary gravitational acceleration into a contact point no larger than a coin.

[IMG_060]
The atmospheric pressure within the cavern collapsed with an explosive sonic implosion, sucking all ambient oxygen toward Caelen's palm.

[IMG_061]
The Goliath's indestructible armor caved inward under twenty tons of localized gravitational pull, crushing its internal organs and skeleton in a fraction of a second.

[IMG_062]
The multi-ton titan collapsed into a dense, compacted sphere of shattered carapace and bone, crashing lifelessly onto the fractured stone floor.

### ACT 4: THE CALM VICTOR & THE ACADEMY DISCIPLINARY RECKONING

[IMG_063]
Suffocating silence descended over the cavern as Lyra and the surviving Class F students stared in petrified disbelief.

[IMG_064]
The Calamity beast that had routed the Academy's top S-Rank prodigy had been erased in three seconds by an F-Rank reject who hadn't even unsheathed a blade.

[IMG_065]
Caelen knelt beside the crushed remains, calmly prying loose a pristine, pulsing violet S-Rank Void Core.

[IMG_066]
In the imperial market, this single dimensional crystal was valued at three thousand Gold Sovereigns—an astronomical fortune sufficient to establish a sovereign research laboratory.

[IMG_067]
He slipped the radiant core into his concealed inner trench-coat pocket, then walked over to the three-ton iron portcullis.

[IMG_068]
With a casual flick of his wrist, Caelen negated the gate's gravitational mass and hoisted the massive iron barrier with a single hand, beckoning the stunned students toward the lift.

[IMG_069]
Up on the surface pavilion, emergency alarm sirens blared as Prince Valen spun a shameless heroic fable to the academy proctors about his tactical sacrifice.

[IMG_070]
The pavilion fell dead silent as the extraction elevator doors parted, revealing Caelen Vance and the entire Class F unit walking out without a single scratch.

[IMG_071]
Head Examiner Keith Morgan pushed through the crowd, his mechanical arm whirring as his analytical gaze locked directly onto Caelen's calm expression.

[IMG_072]
Caelen offered a crisp, polite cadet salute, murmuring that the beast suffered a structural pressure collapse—while in his eyes, the silent sovereign authority of Space and Gravity began to reign.
