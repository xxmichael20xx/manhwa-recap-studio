## 📦 BATCH 01 (Scenes 001 - 024)

[IMG_001] Under the clear twilight sky of year 2025, Qindu University's modern glass-and-steel campus stood peacefully, bathed in the clean warm glow of contemporary LED avenue lights.

[IMG_002] In dormitory Room 402, twenty-year-old university student Lu Chen sat comfortably at his clean birch study desk, resting his cheek against one palm as his sleek laptop displayed an ancient browser game.

[IMG_003] On the bright screen, the retro interface of Heavenly Dao: Infinite Samsara ran quietly, ticking up cultivation numbers with rhythmic, archaic chimes.

[IMG_004] [SYSTEM NOTICE: SIMULATION TIME CONVERGENCE ATTAINED. TOTAL ACCUMULATED CULTIVATION: 1,000,000,000 YEARS.] The brilliant golden notification illuminated the clean white room.

[IMG_005] Across the room, his round-faced roommate Wang Bao was excitedly mashing a mechanical keyboard, shouting over his headset as he tried to clear an online dungeon raid before midnight.

[IMG_006] "Lu Chen, mate, why are you still running that archaic browser fossil? Everyone knows those dead servers are shutting down tonight!" Wang Bao laughed, tossing an empty soda can into the bin.

[IMG_007] Lu Chen offered a faint, unbothered smirk, leaning back in his chair with both hands buried casually in his oversized charcoal-black hoodie pockets. "Ten years of idle background grinding. It would feel rude to close the tab before the developers turn off the lights."

[IMG_008] Suddenly, the entire dormitory room hummed as a resonant primordial vibration rippled through the reinforced concrete foundations.

[IMG_009] The laptop display transformed, lines of ancient gold calligraphy sweeping across the screen as a planetary countdown filled the monitor: 00:09:59.

[IMG_010] [GLOBAL SYSTEM BROADCAST: THE HEAVENLY DAO SIMULATION HAS REACHED MAXIMUM DENSITY. REALITY CONVERGENCE COMMENCING IN TEN MINUTES.]

[IMG_011] Wang Bao blinked, squinting at his smartphone as every app, video feed, and livestream instantly severed connection, replaced by the same glowing golden characters. "What kind of campus hack is this? Is the Computer Science faculty running an elaborate graduation prank?"

[IMG_012] Lu Chen glanced out the fourth-floor glass window into the clear evening sky, his dark obsidian eyes picking up subtle geometric ripples in the atmospheric fabric.

[IMG_013] Above the university library spires, the twilight sky tore open with radiant violet luminescence, revealing an unfathomable cosmic vortex swirling with iridescent spirit qi and celestial starlight.

[IMG_014] The timer plummeted to zero with a concussive chime that echoed directly inside the minds of eight billion humans across the planet.

[IMG_015] In an instant of blinding celestial radiance, the physical dimensions of Blue Star expanded tenfold, the planetary crust fortified by ancient primordial leylines capable of bearing infinite heavenly realms.

[IMG_016] Contemporary glass skyscrapers stood proud on reinforced bedrock as crystalline spirit veins sprouted through city avenues, infusing the clean night air with pure primordial energy.

[IMG_017] [SYSTEM ANNOUNCEMENT: REALITY CONVERGENCE COMPLETED. 10x CONTINENTAL EXPANSION STABILISED. CULTIVATION DATA MANIFESTED INTO PHYSICAL FLESH.] A majestic translucent golden holographic system panel unfolded directly before Lu Chen's desk.

[IMG_018] Wang Bao collapsed backward onto his chair, gasping as brilliant crimson vitality wrapped around his forearms, bulking his frame with sixty-five years of simulated Body Refining strength.

[IMG_019] "Lu Chen! Look at my hands! I can actually feel it! I just lifted our steel bunk bed with one hand like it was made of cardboard!" Wang Bao cheered hysterically, flexing with wild wide-eyed disbelief.

[IMG_020] Meanwhile, Lu Chen simply stretched his shoulders, hearing the soft rustle of his loose hoodie fabric as the unfathomable ocean of one billion years of primordial cultivation settled peacefully within his soul.

[IMG_021] [SYSTEM NOTICE: ACTIVE OUTPUT CALIBRATED TO 25.00% (250,000,000 YEARS) — APPRAISAL STATUS: TRANSCENDENT APEX / INFINITE OVERFLOW.] The golden HUD hovered gracefully in front of his desk inside the clean room.

[IMG_022] "Twenty-five percent is plenty," Lu Chen murmured with a calm, amused deadpan smile. "Two hundred and fifty million years is already enough to turn any high-level calamity into dust with a flick of my finger."

[IMG_023] Through the open doorway, excited shouts echoed down the fourth-floor corridor as university students poured onto the polished ceramic tiles, testing newly awakened qi sparks against the clean walls.

[IMG_024] At the end of the bright corridor, heavy, arrogant footsteps echoed over the commotion, accompanied by the distinct metallic hum of an unsheathed spirit blade.
