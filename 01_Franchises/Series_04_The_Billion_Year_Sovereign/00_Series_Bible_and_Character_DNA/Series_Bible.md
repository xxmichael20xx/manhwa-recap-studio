## 🏛️ Executive Premise & The Convergence

1. **The Inciting Incident:**  
   For ten real-world years, university student **Lu Chen** quietly ran an archaic browser idle cultivation simulator called *Heavenly Dao: Infinite Samsara*. While 99.9% of players quit early, Lu Chen accumulated a simulated **1,000,000,000 Years (One Billion Years)** of primordial cultivation.
2. **The Server Shutdown & Convergence:**  
   A server alert announces the game will shut down in 10 minutes. When the timer hits zero, the game does not delete—it fuses directly with physical reality.
3. **The World Merger & 10x Dimensional Fortitude:**  
   * Blue Star expands **tenfold**, anchored by ancient celestial leylines and primordial crust density.
   * Because physical reality absorbed the entire Heavenly Dao simulation engine, the planet's structural matrix is inherently built to contain all 1,000,000,000 years of cultivation without cracking.
   * Contemporary 2025 university campuses, pristine metropolitan infrastructure, and skyscraper skylines seamlessly blend with crystal-clear primordial spirit energy.
