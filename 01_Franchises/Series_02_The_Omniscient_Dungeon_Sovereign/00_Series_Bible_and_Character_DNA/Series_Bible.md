## 1. Executive Franchise Premise

After serving ten agonizing years as an unawakened luggage porter for the corrupt Aegis Vanguard Guild, Kaelen Thorne is betrayed and thrown into the collapsing S-Rank Obsidian Hollows rift core by aristocratic scion Lord Jason Vane. At the point of death, his latent sovereign genetic protocol unlocks the **$10,000\times$ Neural Calculation Velocity Protocol**, sending him back ten years to Day 1 of his Hunter Assessment.

With a fragile Level 1 biological body incapable of channeling heavy elemental magic or swinging giant blades without neural blowout, Kaelen bypasses guild monopolies by engineering custom **Dead Frontier Heavy Handcannons ("Broncos")** with precision gas venting and tungsten kinetic penetrators. By calculating billions of spatial trajectories, micro-corridor choke points, and destructive waveform phase cancellations ($+A + (-A) = 0$), Kaelen dominates dungeon hazards as a deceptive "F-Rank Carrier", dismantling the noble guild dynasties with pure mathematical certainty.

1. **Zero "Sold Soul to Devil" Slop:** When Kaelen displays anomalous combat feats, onlookers never dismiss them as "demonic witchcraft". Supporting evaluators (Proctor Keith Morgan) deduce facts through **hard sensor data, acoustic telemetry, and compressive stress analysis**.

3. **Act 5+ Progressive Unveiling Law:** Concealing power is strictly a temporary survival phase for early asset accumulation. By the Grand Hunter Academy Tournament (Act 5+), Kaelen openly unleashes dominant tactical capabilities.
