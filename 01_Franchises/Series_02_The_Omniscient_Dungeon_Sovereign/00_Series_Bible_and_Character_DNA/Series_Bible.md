## 🏛️ 1. Executive Summary & Core Premise

In a world governed by the Imperial Hunter Ministry, all youth undergo the official **Awakening Ceremony** at age 18. Class status dictates a citizen's entire future: warriors, mages, and tanks receive million-credit guild signing bonuses, while archers are viewed as fragile, obsolete glass cannons with high ammunition costs and zero melee survivability.

**Caelen Vance**, an impoverished 18-year-old student from District 9, awakens with the mocked class of `[Wood Archer]` and an apparent `[F-Rank: Minor Harvest]` talent—categorised as a useless garbage-collection skill. Humiliated by his arrogant S-Rank classmate Ignis Sterling, Caelen is assigned to the lethal, toxic dungeon refuse pits to clean monster corpses.

However, Caelen's talent is not a low-grade harvesting skill—it is the dormant **Primordial Sovereign Siphon**, an SSS-Tier growth engine operating on three compounding mechanics:
1. **Permanent Stat Accumulation:** Every kill permanently siphons $+1$ to $+3$ random base stats (Strength, Agility, Constitution, Perception, Mana).
2. **Tactical Combat Drain:** Arrows embedded in living enemies drain $10\%$ of their highest stat in real time.
3. **Ghost-String & Soul Trait Extraction:** Weaves ethereal, weightless mana filaments into bowstrings and arrows that bypass armor to strike the mana core, harvesting passive traits like *3km Eagle Eye* and *Silent Trajectory*.

By quietly farming 500 low-tier vermin in the refuse depths, Caelen's physical stats surge past veteran S-Rank hunters. When an unexpected Red Gate mutation threatens his entire graduating class, Caelen draws back his bow with hypersonic kinetic force, releasing orbital-strike arrows that shock the highest military observers in the empire.
