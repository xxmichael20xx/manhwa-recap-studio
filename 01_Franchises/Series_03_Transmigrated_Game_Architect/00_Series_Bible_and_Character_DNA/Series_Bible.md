## 🏛️ 1. Executive Summary & Core Premise

In a cutthroat semi-medieval empire ruled by tyrannical feudal noble houses (with zero magic academies or state schools), deceased Earthlings are transmigrated to the Great Gateway beneath the colossal **100-Floor Tower of Demigods**. Aristocratic oligarchs treat incoming Earthlings as expendable slave labour or cannon fodder, purchasing flashy combat and elemental awakeners for their imperial conquest legions.

**Bramwell Locke ("Bram")**, a 24-year-old former lead VR game architect and systems designer from Earth, awakens with the mocked and dismissed utility talent of `[Common Tool Polisher]`. Evaluated as complete trash by the imperial proctors because he generates zero glowing mana flares or explosive pyrotechnics, Bram is released into the outer slums.

Unbeknownst to the feudal lords, Bram's talent is the universe's ultimate underlying architecture engine: **Uncapped Recursive Enchantment & Semantic Linguistic Refactoring**:
1. **Uncapped Recursive Enhancement:** Bram can grant numerical enhancements ($+N$) to any object, clothing, spell, body part, or living ally without a hard cap. To expand his enhancement cap, he simply cycles enhancements through repeated usage ($100 \times N$ cycles per tier).
2. **The Sovereign Siphon Bank:** Bram can extract and strip enhancements from any enchanted object and store them as liquid numerical currency inside an infinite personal bank. He can instantly withdraw and inject these stored charges into any mundane target in fractions of a second.
3. **Semantic Linguistic Refactoring (2-Word Rewrite):** Bram can rewrite up to two words in the official System name of any item, prop, or skill within the same root category. Crucially, the target's physical appearance remains 100% unchanged, creating the ultimate "sleeper weapon" deceit—a chipped iron knife can possess the cutting sharpness and mass of an abyssal meteorite whilst still looking like 5-copper junk.

Equipped with a friendly smile, rolled-up craftsman sleeves, and a calm, polite demeanor, Bram quietly grinds his cycles in the slums, opening *Locke’s Sundries & Tea*. When arrogant young nobles attempt to extort him or harm his transmigrator neighbours, Bram politely rewrites their legendary swords into chalk and solo-speedruns the Tower of Demigods using ordinary wooden brooms and pocket pebbles.

## 📖 4. The 2-Word Semantic Refactoring Lexicon (Rulebook)

1. **The Category Constraint:** The rewrite must preserve the base root taxonomic class (weapon remains weapon, garment remains garment, liquid remains liquid).
2. **Zero Visual Mutation:** The physical visual texture, scratches, rust, and material weight remain completely unchanged to mundane observation.
3. **Canonical Refactor Table:**
   - `"Rusted Iron Dagger"` $\rightarrow$ `"Rusted [Abyssal Meteorite] Dagger"` (Appearance: rusty kitchen blade; Output: cuts divine metal).
   - `"Imperial Sunfire Rapier"` $\rightarrow$ `"Imperial [Sunfire Chalk]"` (Appearance: glowing golden rapier; Output: crumbles upon impact).
   - `"Imperial Void Sceptre"` $\rightarrow$ `"Imperial [Void Duster]"` (Appearance: dark-matter orb sceptre; Output: harmless bundle of feathers).
   - `"Linen Work Shirt"` $\rightarrow$ `"[Divine Aegis] Shirt"` (Appearance: cheap woven cloth; Output: absolute ballistic deflection).
   - `"Ordinary Pine Needle"` $\rightarrow$ `"[Orbital Ballistic] Needle"` (Appearance: small green leaf; Output: supersonic armor-piercing projectile).
