## 🎙️ Act 1: The Catalyst & Humiliation (0:00 – 3:30)

### Scene 1: The Golden Banquet Hall (0:00 – 1:30)
* **Prompt Tag:** `[IMG_001]`, `[IMG_002]`
* **Voiceover:**
  For three years, Ethan Drake had carried the heaviest burdens of the Vanguard Guild. While the elite strike teams claimed the glory, the press conferences, and the multi-million credit bounties, Ethan walked three paces behind—an unranked core porter whose mana channels had been deemed permanently fractured since birth. He was the invisible mule of Sector Nine... until tonight.

  Across the grand banquet hall of House Vance, beneath crystal chandeliers that mirrored the cold rain beating against the high windows, his loyalty was publicly auctioned. 

  Lady Evelyn Cross did not lower her voice. She did not avert her gaze. With a single, dismissive flick of her manicured fingers, she cast their silver engagement band across the marble floor. The ring bounced against the stone, coming to a dead stop against Ethan's worn combat boots. 

  Standing beside her was Captain Marcus Vance—an A-Rank vanguard clad in bespoke titanium alloy, a predator who commanded the city’s most lucrative dungeon licences. Evelyn stepped seamlessly into Marcus’s shadow, trading three years of shared survival for the security of a noble bloodline.

### Scene 2: Cast Into the Lower City (1:30 – 2:30)
* **Prompt Tag:** `[IMG_003]`, `[IMG_004]`
* **Voiceover:**
  Marcus did not draw his blade. In the upper circles of the Awakened nobility, physical violence was uncouth when social annihilation was so effortless. With a single gesture to the guild bailiffs, Marcus revoked Ethan’s porter licence on grounds of physical deficiency, stripping him of his guild stipend and his right to enter licensed gate facilities.

  Cast out into the neon-drenched alleys of the Lower District, the cold rain soaked through Ethan’s frayed trench-coat. Every step felt like wading through lead. His mana core throbbed with a dull, sickening ache—the familiar burning sensation of a biological system running on empty. He was twenty-two, penniless, and marked as dead weight by the most powerful syndicate in the capital.

### Scene 3: The Crimson Awakening (2:30 – 3:30)
* **Prompt Tag:** `[IMG_005]`, `[IMG_006]`
* **Voiceover:**
  In a forgotten service corridor beneath the monorail tracks, Ethan’s knees finally buckled. His vital signs cratered. His vision tunnelled into pitch darkness as his heart rhythm stuttered to a near standstill. 

  Two percent vitality. The threshold where mortal biology ceases.

  Yet, at the absolute edge of clinical death, the ambient air grew motionless. The sound of pouring rain halted in mid-air. A crimson interface, forged from dark obsidian glass, crystallised directly before his retinas.

  **[Emergency Protocol Initialised: Host Biological Failure Imminent.]**  
  **[Celestial Core Diagnostics Complete. Dormant Sovereign Protocol: Synchronised.]**

  Unlike the fragile blue interfaces issued by the International Hunter Ministry, this system bore no rank limits. No guild oversight. A cascade of warm, liquid fire surged outward from his fractured spine, burning away the necrotic tissue of his ruined channels. 

  His notification log flashed a single baseline calibration:  
  **[Host Physical Attributes Re-Indexed: Base Constitution Allocated: Two Hundred Points.]**

## 🎙️ Act 2: The Hidden Trial & Durability Calibration (3:30 – 10:00)

### Scene 4: Descending into Sector Nine Catacombs (3:30 – 5:30)
* **Prompt Tag:** `[IMG_007]`, `[IMG_008]`
* **Voiceover:**
  Ethan rose to his feet. The persistent ache that had plagued his chest for seven years had vanished. In its place was a dense, heavy stillness—a weight distribution within his muscles that felt less like biological tissue and more like forged tungsten.

  The system displayed a strict requirement:  
  **[Current Level: 1. Vessel Saturation: Zero of Five Thousand Experience Points.]**

  To understand what two hundred points in Constitution truly meant, he needed a proving ground where civilian eyes could not follow. Sector Nine harboured an unregistered sewer fissure—a low-tier sub-gate that the municipal authorities had deemed too unprofitable to purge. 

  Dropping through the rusted maintenance hatch, Ethan landed in the subterranean catacombs. The air reeked of damp sulphur and old rot. In the shadows ahead, three pairs of venomous crimson eyes ignited within the gloom.

---

### Scene 5: The Physical Threshold Test (5:30 – 7:30)
* **Prompt Tag:** `[IMG_009]`, `[IMG_010]`
* **Voiceover:**
  A Level Two Shadowfang Hound—a beast engineered for ambushing civilian scavengers. With a guttural snarl, the alpha lunged across twenty paces, its serrated fangs aimed directly for Ethan’s jugular.

  Ordinary instincts would have screamed to dodge. But Ethan stood completely still, his eyes tracking the creature's trajectory. He raised his bare left forearm to intercept the strike.

  *CRACK.*

  The sound that echoed through the stone corridor was not the tearing of flesh. It was the sharp, violent snap of bone breaking against solid steel. 

  The beast’s upper jaw shattered on contact. Its fangs splintered into fragments, scattering across the wet flags like cracked porcelain. To a creature possessing a combined physical strength of barely fifteen points, clamping down on Ethan’s two-hundred Constitution was equivalent to biting an industrial anvil.

  Ethan felt nothing more than a dull kinetic vibration against his skin. Zero penetration. Zero blood.

  Stepping into the pocket, Ethan drove a casual right palm into the creature's chest plate. 

  **[Direct Kinetic Impact: One Hundred Percent Force Transfer.]**

  The impact shockwave blew out the back of the beast’s ribcage, sending the carcass hurtling twenty metres down the tunnel before collapsing in a heap of dust and bone.

  **[Target Neutralised. Experience Acquired: +250 EXP. Progress: 250 / 5,000.]**

---

### Scene 6: The Neural Download & Kinetic Kata (7:30 – 10:00)
* **Prompt Tag:** `[IMG_011]`, `[IMG_012]`
* **Voiceover:**
  Within the remains of the alpha hound lay an unformed skill orb—a rarity in low-tier sub-gates. 

  **[Skill Manual Detected: Kinetic Resonance (Passive Tier 1).]**  
  **[Absorb Neural Data?]**

  Ethan pressed his palm against the crystal. The orb dissolved into a sudden cascade of electrical telemetry, surging through his optic nerves and slamming into his motor cortex. 

  This was not the painless, cartoonish mastery promised in low-grade guild manuals. Ethan gritted his teeth as his brain violently integrated ten thousand repetitions of rotational footwork, impact mitigation, and kinetic dispersion. A thin bead of sweat traced his jawline. His muscular fibres twitched in involuntary spasms as his nervous system recalibrated to the foreign motor memory.

  Thirty seconds of agony... followed by absolute clarity. 

  Every punch he threw was no longer just raw mass. Kinetic Resonance transformed his natural bodily durability into an offensive weapon: whenever an opponent struck his flesh, the dissipated kinetic energy would be stored and discharged through his next counter-strike with double the force.

## 🎙️ Act 3: Choke-Point Tactical Escalation (10:00 – 16:30)

### Scene 7: The Syndicate Scavenger Ambush (10:00 – 12:30)
* **Prompt Tag:** `[IMG_013]`, `[IMG_014]`
* **Voiceover:**
  By the fourth hour of his expedition, the subterranean passage opened into an ancient drainage cistern. Ethan’s kill counter stood at eighteen hounds, his experience pool reaching three thousand two hundred points.

  A sharp whistling sound split the silence.

  Ethan tilted his head two inches to the left. A hardened steel crossbow bolt hissed past his ear, embedding itself four inches into the granite masonry behind him.

  From the perimeter shadows stepped fifteen men—the Iron Jackals, a notorious syndicate of black-market scavengers who preyed on unlicensed porters in the lower catacombs. Their leader, a scarred veteran named Grigor, wielded a pneumatically powered siege hammer, flanked by riflemen holding the high ground on the rusted iron catwalks above.

  *"Well, well,"* Grigor chuckled, tapping his hammer against the iron railing. *"Vanguard Guild’s little trash-heap decided to go hunting all by himself. Hand over the cores, boy, and we might leave you with enough fingers to crawl back to the gutter."*

---

### Scene 8: Choke-Point Annihilation (12:30 – 14:30)
* **Prompt Tag:** `[IMG_015]`, `[IMG_016]`
* **Voiceover:**
  They did not attack one by one. The Iron Jackals were ruthless professionals: on Grigor’s hand signal, the four riflemen on the catwalk opened fire simultaneously, while the melee squad advanced in a disciplined interlocking wedge.

  Ethan did not attempt a reckless open-field charge. Instead, he snapped backward on his heels, retreating six metres into the narrow mouth of the drainage conduit. 

  In a three-metre wide corridor, fifteen men meant nothing. Their numerical advantage collapsed into a bottleneck where only two men could engage at a time, completely obstructing the riflemen's line of fire above.

  The first two syndicate enforcers lunged forward with electric stun batons. Ethan stepped into their guard. Catching both batons with his bare hands, the high-voltage electrical discharge sizzled uselessly against his high-density skin. 

  Twisting his wrists, he drove the two enforcers together with sickening force. The concussive collision pulverised their reinforced chest rigs, dropping both men instantly.

  Grigor dropped from the catwalk, his pneumatic hammer shrieking as its pressure chambers fired at maximum velocity. The warhammer descended like a falling meteorite directly toward Ethan's cranium.

  Ethan didn't duck. He raised his open right palm.

  *BOOM.*

  The kinetic shockwave blew out the subterranean water pipes, showering the tunnel in high-pressure mist. But Ethan’s arm did not yield a single millimetre. The massive steel warhammer was halted dead in mid-flight, trapped in the vice-like grip of Ethan’s bare fingers.

---

### Scene 9: Calculated Finality (14:30 – 16:30)
* **Prompt Tag:** `[IMG_017]`, `[IMG_018]`
* **Voiceover:**
  Grigor’s eyes bulged with primal terror. The pneumatic gauge on his hammer hissed violently, but the metal face had begun to cave inward under the sheer compressive pressure of Ethan’s grip.

  *"Who... what are you?!"* Grigor stammered, frantically trying to dislodge the weapon. *"We work for House Vance! Touch me, and the Captain will hunt your entire bloodline!"*

  Ethan’s expression remained carved from ice. Arrogance backed by a noble family’s name was currency in the upper city. Down here, in the cold dark beneath the streets, names held zero kinetic mass.

  Ethan discharged the kinetic energy stored from the hammer's impact. 

  **[Kinetic Resonance Discharged.]**

  A focused shockwave erupted from Ethan's palm, tearing through the hammer's casing and blasting Grigor through the brick wall behind him. The remaining scavengers dropped their rifles onto the wet stones, scrambling over each other in blind panic to escape the corridor.

  Ethan did not pursue them. Survival in Sector Nine was about efficiency, not ego.

  The violence in the conduit had awakened the true master of the sub-gate. 

  Dropping from the ceiling of the central reservoir fell an Obsidian Behemoth—a mutated Level Five gate guardian clad in mineralized basalt armour. A creature that normally required an eight-man guild subjugation party.

  **[Boss Encounter: Obsidian Behemoth (Level 5).]**  
  **[Defeat Guardian to Finalise Initial Vessel Calibration.]**

  The monster roared, swinging a club of solid stone. Ethan met it head-on. Evading the crushing primary swing with the footwork etched into his motor cortex, Ethan closed the distance in a blur of acceleration. 

  Channelling the full rotational force of his two-hundred Constitution, he drove a single straight punch into the glowing violet mana core resting at the centre of the creature’s chest.

  *SHATTER.*

  The mineralized core exploded into brilliant violet shards. The massive stone colossus froze mid-stride, hairline fractures racing across its granite limbs before disintegrating into a rain of inert gravel.

  **[Boss Neutralised. Experience Acquired: +1,800 EXP.]**  
  **[Total Experience: 5,000 / 5,000. Vessel Saturation Reached.]**
