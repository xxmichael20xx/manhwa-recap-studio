## 3. Universal Anti-Semantic Collision & Entity Bleed Protocol

To prevent cross-attention bleeding (e.g. character names morphing into environmental nouns or monster species):

1. **Strict Entity Disambiguation:**
   - Every monster species must use distinct biological and taxonomic descriptors:
     - `Shadowfang Hound`: Described exclusively as a `feral horned quadruped shadow-beast`.
     - `Obsidian Behemoth`: Described exclusively as a `colossal subterranean crystalline stone titan`.
     - `Syndicate Scavengers`: Described exclusively as `humanoid looters in rusted metal scrap armor`.
     - `Grigor`: Described exclusively as a `heavy brute scavenger boss with hydraulic prosthetic arm`.
2. **Noun Overlap Prohibition:**
   - Never use character surnames as verbs, objects, or environmental elements (e.g. never prompt `"a glowing cross"` when Evelyn Cross is present; use `"a luminous four-pointed glyph"`).
3. **Syntactic Clause Isolation:**
   - Prompts must be structured in comma-separated modular clauses:
     `[Flow Character Name], [Shot Framing & Composition], [Subject Action & Pose], [Environment Details], [Widescreen & Art Style Tail]`.
