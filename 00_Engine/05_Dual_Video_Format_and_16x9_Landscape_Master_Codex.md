## 🏛️ The 4 Core 16:9 Landscape Composition Archetypes
All 16:9 visual prompt matrices must strictly adhere to the 4 horizontal composition archetypes:

1. **Archetype A: Wide Cinematic Battleground (~40% of Deck)**
   - **Composition:** Protagonist on the left third with glowing eyes/blade, charging monster horde on the right third, deep dungeon vanishing point in the background.
   - **Motion:** `pan_left_to_right` (Constant horizontal sweep across the battlefront).
   - **Syntax:** `Cinematic wide-angle manhwa composition, 16:9 landscape aspect ratio. Protagonist [Character DNA] standing in the left foreground with glowing weapon, facing a colossal charging monster horde on the right cavern wall. Dark fantasy action manhwa art style, sharp ink linework, high contrast cel shading, volumetric lighting, horizontal 16:9, textless manhwa artwork.`

2. **Archetype B: In-Frame Holographic System HUD (~25% of Deck)**
   - **Composition:** Protagonist in mid-shot beside a floating translucent blue/gold status window displaying clean RPG metrics ($10,000\times\text{ Calculation Velocity}$, $+15\text{ STR}$).
   - **Motion:** `hero_zoom_in` (Subtle center push-in $1.0\times \rightarrow 1.05\times$).
   - **Syntax:** `16:9 widescreen manhwa composition. Protagonist standing in the right foreground, observing a glowing translucent holographic status interface floating in mid-air on the left displaying golden runic RPG metrics. Dark fantasy action manhwa art style, sharp linework, vibrant anime lighting, horizontal 16:9, textless manhwa artwork.`

3. **Archetype C: Dynamic Kinetic Clash & Spatial Shears (~20% of Deck)**
   - **Composition:** Fast horizontal action trajectory, sonic shockwaves, zero-mana parries, high-velocity sword trail spanning the widescreen frame.
   - **Motion:** `pan_diagonal_sweep` or `pan_right_to_left`.
   - **Syntax:** `Dynamic widescreen action manhwa shot, 16:9 horizontal. Protagonist dashing horizontally across the frame with supreme velocity, leaving a blazing cyan mana arc that cleaves the armored dungeon boss in two. Dark fantasy action manhwa art style, intense dynamic lighting, horizontal 16:9.`

4. **Archetype D: Vast Panoramic Establishing Shot (~15% of Deck)**
   - **Composition:** Monumental environmental depth (Imperial Catacombs, colossal Red Gates, volcanic cavern chasms).
   - **Motion:** `hero_zoom_out` or `pan_top_to_bottom`.
   - **Syntax:** `Vast panoramic landscape establishing shot, 16:9 aspect ratio. Colossal shattered crystal dungeon cathedral stretching into the volcanic abyss, towering obsidian pillars, tiny hunter silhouettes in distance. Dark fantasy action manhwa art style, volumetric atmospheric lighting, horizontal 16:9.`
