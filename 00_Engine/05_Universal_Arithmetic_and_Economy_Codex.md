## 1. Monetary Currency & Purchasing Power (Imperial Sovereign Standard)

All series operating within the Studio Engine must adhere to a grounded 3-tier metallic currency system pegged to real purchasing power. Arbitrary monetary inflation (e.g. trillions of gold) is strictly prohibited.

| Currency Tier | Symbol | Ratio / Value | Equivalent Purchasing Power | Common In-World Transactions |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Copper** | `cp` | $1\text{ cp} \approx \text{£1.00}$ | Low-value daily subsistence | Street food (5 cp), subway/transit fare (2 cp), basic field rations (10 cp). |
| **Silver** | `sp` | $1\text{ sp} = 100\text{ cp} \approx \text{£100}$ | Trade & standard living | Academy textbooks (3 sp), iron sword/dagger (15 sp), monthly student rent (50 sp). |
| **Gold Sovereign** | `gp` | $1\text{ gp} = 100\text{ sp} \approx \text{£10,000}$ | Institutional & high-tier | Grade-1 enchanted armor (5 gp), Academy semester tuition (50 gp), Rift license (250 gp). |

## 2. Dungeon Monster Core Bounty Scale

Dungeon cores serve as the primary industrial energy resource and monetary standard:

- **F-Rank Core:** $20 – 40\text{ sp}$ *(~£2,000 – £4,000; low-tier porter/scavenger day wage)*
- **E-Rank Core:** $1 – 2\text{ gp}$ *(~£10,000 – £20,000; standard military squad bounty)*
- **D-Rank Core:** $5 – 8\text{ gp}$ *(~£50,000 – £80,000; minor dungeon sub-boss)*
- **C-Rank Core:** $25 – 40\text{ gp}$ *(~£250,000 – £400,000; full guild raid split)*
- **B-Rank Core:** $100 – 150\text{ gp}$ *(~£1,000,000 – £1,500,000; strategic industrial resource)*
- **A-Rank Core:** $500 – 800\text{ gp}$ *(~£5,000,000 – £8,000,000; city-level defense asset)*
- **S-Rank Core:** $3,000 – 5,000\text{ gp}$ *(~£30,000,000 – £50,000,000; national treasury grade)*

## 3. Experience (EXP) Curve & Progression Saturation

Levels are capped at **Level 100** (the historical physiological barrier of human biology). EXP is earned through genuine combat stress and monster core absorption:

| Level Tier | Classification | EXP per Level | Monster Kill Equivalent |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Lv 1 – 10** | **Cadet / Novice** | $500 \rightarrow 1,200\text{ EXP}$ | 10–15 F-Rank beasts or 2 E-Rank beasts |
| **Lv 11 – 25** | **Vanguard / D-Rank** | $2,500 \rightarrow 6,000\text{ EXP}$ | 20 D-Rank beasts or 1 Dungeon Boss |
| **Lv 26 – 50** | **Elite / C–B Rank** | $12,000 \rightarrow 30,000\text{ EXP}$ | Frontline dimensional rift campaigns |
| **Lv 51 – 75** | **High Commander / A-Rank** | $60,000 \rightarrow 150,000\text{ EXP}$ | S-Rank dungeon raid participation |
| **Lv 76 – 100** | **National Sovereign / S-Rank** | $300,000 \rightarrow 1,000,000\text{ EXP}$ | Cataclysm rift anomalies (Historical Elite) |

## 4. Attribute Baseline & Combat Physics

- **Human Baseline:** Standard adult human attribute score is **10**.
- **Stat Point Allocation:** Exactly **$+3\text{ points}$** awarded per level up.
- **Absolute Durability Rule:**
  $$\text{Effective Damage} = \max(0, \text{Incoming Kinetic/Magical Energy} - (\text{Constitution} \times \text{Density Factor}))$$
  When $Attack - Defence \le 0 \implies 0\text{ Damage}$. Low-tier mundane strikes shatter or deflect without inflicting damage on high-CON hunters.
