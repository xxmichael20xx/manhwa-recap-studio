## 🔒 The 8 Aligned Core Pillars (The 70/30 Calibrated Hybrid Standard)

Whenever adapting, rewriting, or narrating content across ANY franchise or episode, all agents MUST enforce:

1. **Re-Indexed Progression Arithmetic (Clean RPG Math):** Never parrot bloated, arbitrary numbers ($100\text{M EXP}$). Recalibrate progression into clean, predictable intervals (Level 1 $\rightarrow$ 10 requires $5,000\text{ EXP}$; stats scale predictably from 10 to $200+$).
2. **Absolute Physical Durability Threshold ($Attack - Defence \le 0 \implies 0\text{ Dmg}$):** Superhuman Constitution ($150–200+$) provides physical invulnerability to mundane fodder (blades deflect, teeth snap). Use **Vessel Calibration Latency** only if an injury is required for subsequent plot progression.
3. **Dynamic Combat Spectrum (Tactical Realism with Fodder Curb-Stomps):** High-tactical realism (choke-points, elevation, kinetic dampening) is the default for major bosses and elite clashes. However, ~30% of combat encounters can feature overconfident, reckless, or slightly dim fodder mobs for satisfying, breezy curb-stomps.
4. **Tiered Antagonist Hierarchy & Classic Face-Slap Dynamics:** High-level guild oligarchs and masterminds act with calculated self-preservation. However, low-tier arrogant bullies, corrupt examiners, and rival scions can openly mock and underestimate the MC to establish classic manhwa underdog catharsis, while secret higher-ups in VIP booths recognize the MC's true terrifying foundation.
5. **Balanced Institutional Realism:** Ground world events with contextual explanations for Hunter Ministry media blackouts, property damage treaties, and dimensional atmospheric density.
6. **Flexible Narrative Resolution & Suspense Pacing (Minimum 5-Batch Arcs):** Tensions, accusations, or forbidden skill investigations must NOT be resolved immediately. Allow misunderstandings, suspicious mana signatures, and institutional curiosity to simmer across a **minimum of 5 batches (120+ scenes / multi-arc progression)** before the decisive truth or grand face-slap payoff is unveiled.
7. **Operator-Prompted Concealment & Strategic Sandbagging:** The protagonist is permitted to conceal, feign weakness, or selectively throttle their abilities based on tactical need (e.g. scoring just enough to pass an exam or sandbagging to draw out an assassin). The AI agent will proactively ask the operator during pre-flight alignment when the MC's grand unveiling should take place.
8. **Contextual Evaluation & Proctor Dynamics:** Practical exams and trials adapt to the plot: official state trials can feature active live-feed surveillance, while underground or corrupt trials can feature oblivious or dismissive examiners setting up major shock revelations.
9. **Mandatory World Setting Visual Selection Gate:** Whenever drafting master narrative acts, world geography, or setting foundations for a new series or arc, the agent MUST present at least 3 distinct image-generated concept variations (in the locked series aspect ratio, e.g. 16:9 Landscape) depicting the architectural palette, climate, and lighting mood. The agent is strictly forbidden from locking or finalizing narrative setting lore until the operator reviews and explicitly selects their preferred world setting visual variation.

## ⚡ The 4 High-Retention Script & Voiceover Directives

1. **Strict Linear Chronological Continuity (Zero In Medias Res / Flash-Forward Loops):**
   - **Absolute Ban on Flash-Forward Cold Opens:** Strictly forbidden from opening episodic scripts with flash-forwards to future crises (*in medias res*, *"X Days Earlier..."*, *"12 Hours Earlier..."*). In multi-episode marathon and omnibus compilations, out-of-order timeline jumps break continuous viewer immersion and create disjointed narrative loops.
   - **Seamless 1:1 Continuation:** Every subsequent episode MUST resume directly, chronologically, and seamlessly from the exact terminal scene, dialogue beat, or cliffhanger action of the preceding episode.
2. **Punchy, Non-Technical System HUD Notifications:**
   - Spoken holographic system notices must be crisp, authoritative, and under 12 words without heavy academic or physics jargon (e.g. `[SYSTEM: CALCULATION VELOCITY 10,000x. ENEMY WEAKNESS LOCATED.]`, `[SYSTEM NOTICE: VESSEL BREAKTHROUGH COMPLETED. CONSTITUTION +50.]`).
3. **Deadpan Sarcasm & Charismatic MC Dialogue:**
   - Make the protagonist deeply likable and memorable by giving them unbothered, witty, deadpan comebacks when facing arrogant bullies or impossible monsters (e.g. *"You spent thirty minutes bragging about an iron dagger?"*, *"I asked for a challenge, not your life story."*).
4. **Multi-Episode Binge Hooks (The Ticking Clock Cliffhanger):**
   - Conclude every episode with an unresolved external threat or looming crisis in the final 30–45 seconds (e.g. a Red Gate opening above the city, or an S-Rank Enforcement Squad dispatched to arrest the MC) to drive immediate binge-watching into the next episode.
5. **Contextual Crowd & Public Atmosphere Rule:**
   - When writing academy, guild, or public city scenes, actively weave in natural peer reactions, whispers, murmurs, and public onlookers to establish authentic world life and enhance underdog face-slap catharsis.
   - Distinguish between public shared spaces (where background onlookers add vital realism) and private sanctuary spaces (such as private workshops, bedchambers, or sanctums, where isolation reinforces intimacy and tactical focus).
6. **Mandatory 1:1 Series Bible Multi-Episode Arc Anchor:**
   - Every episodic script MUST anchor its narrative acts 1:1 to an approved Arc in `00_Series_Bible_and_Character_DNA/Series_Bible.md`. An Arc spans multiple episodes (typically 3–6 episodes per arc; e.g. Season 1 = 18 episodes across 4 Arcs). Strictly forbidden from rushing or completing an entire Arc in 1–2 episodes.
   - Prior to drafting, cross-examine the target episode with the bible's `## 🗺️ Narrative Arcs & Multi-Climax Structure` to verify the setup, rising action, midpoint turn, and climax confrontation.
   - Strictly forbidden from inventing unapproved plot arcs or divergent storylines outside the Series Bible.
7. **Mandatory Episodic Shorts & Climax Tagging Standard (Multi-Shorts Architecture):**
   - Every episode MUST feature at least 1 (and optionally 2–3 for long-form episodes) explicitly tagged 45–56s viral hook sequences directly in `01_Episode_Script.md`:
     * `<!-- CLIMAX_START -->` / `<!-- CLIMAX_END -->`: Peak climactic face-slap, boss cleave, or breakthrough.
     * `<!-- SHORT_EARLY_START -->` / `<!-- SHORT_EARLY_END -->` (or `<!-- SHORT_2_START -->`): Underdog humiliation-to-awakening or public appraisal shock.
   - Tagging enables deterministic extraction by the studio compiler so the operator can post multiple viral shorts per episode across TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
8. **The Zero-At-Token Narration Law (Absolute Prohibition on @ in Spoken Scripts):**
   - All narrative scripts (`01_Episode_Script.md`) and `<narrative_sync>` clauses MUST use natural character and entity names with ZERO `@` or `@{...}` tokens.
   - Never inject prompt-conditioning tags (`@{Protagonist}`) into narration; TTS voiceover models pronounce `@` as *"at"*, resulting in corrupted voice audio.
9. **The Double-Blind Alias Law (Stele & Global Leaderboard Climax Integrity):**
   - When world monuments, divine ranking steles, or global leaderboards unveil the protagonist's rank, public world displays must display their Dao Title / System Alias (e.g. `XIAO YAO`), explicitly shattering any attempt to carve their mortal name into `[REAL IDENTITY: REDACTED BY PRIMORDIAL SOVEREIGN AUTHORITY]`.
   - The protagonist's true identity is confirmed privately to the audience via their floating System HUD (`@{Heavenly Dao Simulator Interface}`), maintaining public suspense and investigative pursuit across the arc while delivering total viewer catharsis.

10. **The Continuous Narrative & Zero Fourth-Wall Invariant:**
    - Spoken scripts (`01_Episode_Script.md`), voiceovers, dialogue, and prompt `<narrative_sync>` blocks are **STRICTLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY FORBIDDEN** from mentioning production or scheduling terms such as *"Episode 1"*, *"Episode 2"*, *"Next Episode"*, *"Next Chapter"*, or *"Stay tuned"*.
    - **Strict Ban on Calendar / Fourth-Wall Time Breaks:** Strictly forbidden from using broadcast phrases like *"Next week..."*, *"Tune in next time..."*, or *"In the upcoming episode..."*. All narrative transitions must use organic, in-world phrasing (*"in a few days"*, *"our next phase"*, *"tomorrow morning"*).
    - **Strict Ban on Rigid Game-Engine Percentages ("50% Development"):** Strictly forbidden from describing fantasy worlds with arbitrary numeric progress labels like *"stuck at 50% development"*. Describe the world through organic human realities: *"centuries of technological stagnation"*, *"foundations left in ruins"*, or *"a kingdom that hoards high magic while ordinary people lack clean water"*.

11. **The Canonical Family Naming & Pre-Registration Law (Anti-Anonymous-Family Mandate):**
    - Whenever an episode introduces, mentions, or depicts family members (parents, siblings, children, grandparents, aunt, uncle, clan kin):
      * **Explicit Canonical Naming:** The script MUST name them with explicit canonical names (e.g. *Garrett Miller*, *Clara Miller*, *Sora Chen*), never vague generic placeholders (*"his mother"*, *"the father"*, *"a sister"*).
      * **Vault Pre-Registration:** Every named family member MUST be pre-registered in `character_vault/characters.json` and have their master reference plate generated BEFORE prompt matrix assembly.

## 🧠 The Lead Lore Auditor & 5 Logic Gates (Anti-Brain-Dead Standard)

1. **The "1,000-Year Brain-Dead Civilization" Ban:**
   - Ancient mages and knights never fail for centuries simply because they "didn't think of" a mundane trick (e.g. using a mirror or dropping dirt).
   - If common objects are used by the protagonist to overcome calamity threats, the script MUST explicitly ground the success in the protagonist's unique numerical arithmetic engine (e.g. $+5,000$ liquid enhancement). The world's inhabitants must never be portrayed as low-IQ toddlers.
2. **The Credible Motive & Anti-Trauma-Dump Mandate:**
   - Powerful military commanders, guild masters, or noble antagonists NEVER reveal classified state secrets or sensitive political weaknesses to a commoner without a calculated motive (e.g. clandestine sting, psychological probe, or coercive interrogation).
3. **The Anti-Deus-Ex-Machina Admin Gate:**
   - Ancient AI systems, alien hypervisors, or divine wardens NEVER magically recognize an Earthling transmigrator as "The Creator" or hand over root admin passwords. System access must be earned through engineering deduction, buffer overflows, or reverse-engineering heuristics.
4. **The Physical Gateway & Leaderboard Surveillance Gate:**
   - When public steles or guild bells broadcast record-breaking clears across a capital city, the narrative MUST explicitly account for how the protagonist bypasses physical entrance ambushes and Imperial Watch cordons (e.g. private transit runes refactored into backdoor slum anchors).
5. **The Active External Ticking Clock (The "Why Not Quit?" Defense):**
   - Whenever an ultimate dungeon clear threatens global destruction, the narrative MUST establish an active, inescapable external crisis (e.g. container entropy memory leak causing a crash within 90 days, or a corrupt noble blood-core siege) proving why the protagonist cannot simply walk away.

## 🎙️ Spoken-Prose & IP Sanitisation Standard

1. **Absolute Ban on Third-Party Trademarks:** Zero real-world titles, commercial brands, or games (no *"Solo Leveling"*, *"Glock"*, *"Dark Souls"*). Use 100% original in-universe terminology.
2. **Absolute Ban on Mathematical LaTeX & Comparison Operators:** Strictly forbidden from writing algebraic equations (e.g. `(+A + (-A) = 0)`), raw LaTeX (`$...$`), comparison operators (`<=`, `>=`), or raw plus/minus signs (`+3`) inside spoken narrative blocks. Convey through natural, immersive narrative prose.
3. **Absolute Ban on Meta-Jargon:** Strictly forbidden from allowing internal architectural terms (*"Studio Engine"*, *"Invariant"*, *"Benchmark"*, *"Codex"*) into spoken narrative text.
4. **Zero-Bleed Script Formatting:** Header metadata and Act summary rows in `01_Episode_Script.md` must NEVER contain bracketed `[IMG_...]` tags. Only physical narrative scene cuts may start with `[IMG_XXX]` tags at the beginning of narrative lines.
5. **The Strict 1:1 Spoken Clause-to-Plate Invariant:**
   - Every `[IMG_XXX]` tag MUST precede the exact, immediate spoken clause where that visual action begins.
   - Never place tags before introductory or transitional clauses that belong to a different plate.
   - Split compound multi-action sentences into separate sentences with dedicated `[IMG_XXX]` tags.
6. **The Audio-Visual Boundary Isolation Mandate (Zero Bleed Script Law):**
   - Every `[IMG_XXX]` scene cue must describe an independent, visually distinct physical beat that matches the exact spoken audio duration of that sentence.
   - Narrative actions must not bleed across sentences. If Scene A narrates walking up to a building and Scene B narrates pushing open the door, Scene A must NEVER prompt the character inside the building.
   - Split compound run-on sentences into clean single-action clauses to give the storyboard artist and image generator unambiguous 1:1 visual anchors.

## 🧠 The 6 Master Laws of Narrative Common Sense & Physical Continuity

1. **The Milestone Wardrobe Shift Law (Zero Pop-In / Zero Premature Reversion):**
   - When a narrative milestone awards or establishes crafted attire (e.g. Michael hunting timber wolves and crafting tailored wolf-leather clothing with a fur collar at age 7), that wardrobe becomes the character's **permanent canonical model**.
   - When departing a sanctuary or travelling to civilization, the character **already wears that attire upon stepping outside**.
   - The agent is strictly forbidden from reverting to previous rags/linens or introducing the new attire abruptly in a single scene. The wardrobe stays locked across all subsequent episodes unless explicitly changed by a new narrative event.

2. **The Physical Prop Lifecycle State Machine (Absolute Persistence Law):**
   - If a character equips or packs an item to transport goods (e.g. packing rare herbs into an `Herb Rucksack`), that prop is physically bound to their character model across **100% of sequential transit, arrival, gate, and market scenes**.
   - Props cannot vanish into thin air between panels or morph into different items (e.g. a rucksack turning into two satchels).
   - The prop remains visible until an explicit narrative action states they put it down (e.g. unstrapping the rucksack onto Rowan's shop counter).

3. **Anti-Template Contamination (Context-Matched Prose Law):**
   - Layout templates (Tier B Dual-Split, Tier C Multi-Panel) must **never** inject generic combat boilerplate (*"high-impact kinetic technique motion"*, *"decisive clash"*, *"physical impact shockwave"*) into peaceful scenes.
   - For peaceful scenes (entering an apothecary, browsing market stalls, drinking tea), templates must strictly use calm narrative prose (*"gentle door chime"*, *"polite bow"*, *"examining dried roots"*).

4. **Negative Metaphor Diegetic Shield (Literalism Purge Law):**
   - Narrative figures of speech that describe what a character is **not** doing (*"zero magic wand"*, *"without chanting"*, *"didn't need a sword"*) must NEVER leave the negative keywords in prompt blocks.
   - Diffusion models cannot parse negation and will draw the forbidden object. All negative idioms must be diegetically translated to literal positive actions (e.g. *"bare knuckles crackling with concentrated cyan mana, tapping the stone with empty hands"*).

5. **Vertical Observer Staging & Separation of Agency Law:**
   - When an observer watches external events from a higher elevation (e.g. a 2nd-floor bedroom window or balcony), the camera MUST be staged inside the window framing the observer in the foreground looking down.
   - External crowd focus (e.g. students cheering noble student Vance in the courtyard) must NEVER be bound to the protagonist. The protagonist remains isolated upstairs as a detached observer with a dry smirk.

6. **Continuous Entity Reference & Weapon Binding Law:**
   - In multi-beat combat clashes, signature weapons (`@{Carved Blackwood Staff}`) and boss entities (`@{Calamity Abyssal Wyrm}`) must remain in `<character_refs>` and prompt descriptions across **100% of consecutive clash panels**.
   - Dropping an entity reference between beats causes diffusion models to hallucinate generic ogres, raw tree trunks, or random wands.

7. **The Two-Character Dialogue Anchor Law (Anti-Stranger Hallucination Mandate):**
   - Whenever two characters engage in dialogue, trading, or mutual reaction across a table, counter, or room (e.g. Michael and Rowan in the apothecary), both the script beats and the prompt matrix must explicitly identify and anchor both parties.
   - Dual-panel split strips (Tier B) depicting an exchange must stage the active speaker in the upper panel and the explicitly tagged conversational partner reacting in the lower panel, ensuring diffusion models attach both character reference plates and never hallucinate an unanchored, inconsistent stranger.

8. **Signature Weapon Canonical Morphology (Anti-Twisting Staff Law):**
   - Signature weapons like the *Carved Blackwood Staff* are strictly straight, smooth, cylindrical solid wooden bo quarterstaffs with flat or iron-capped ends. The script and prompt descriptions must explicitly prohibit wizard orbs, crystal tops, or gnarled crooked roots.

## 🌍 The 5 Slow-Burn Worldbuilding & Crafting Realism Laws

1. **Multi-Batch Phase Expansion (Zero `1 Batch = 1 Story Point` Rushing):**
   - Never compress foundational lore (why the protagonist was chosen, parent/clan history, magic mechanics, or how a real-world craft works) into a few lines or a single 24-scene batch.
   - Expand each worldbuilding and crafting phase across **2 to 4+ batches (48–96 scenes)**, explaining *why* the world's current method fails, *what* natural valley/local resources are used, and *how* each step is tested and built.
2. **Always-On Sentient AI / Spirit Best-Friend Co-Pilot Law:**
   - When a protagonist has a sentient AI or soul-bound companion (e.g. `@{LUMINA}`), treat them as a **lifelong best friend and constant active co-pilot**—never a passive notification box that only appears occasionally.
   - Post-awakening, the companion must converse with the protagonist in their mind and accompany almost every single action, analysis, and banter beat (strictly without referencing third-party pop-culture trademarks like "Jarvis" or "Iron Man" in scripts or prompts).
3. **Child Vessel Mental Overload (Awakening Fever) & Live-Observation Affinity Gate:**
   - Merging adult past-life memories and a massive AI knowledge/magic processor into a young child's brain must trigger a realistic **Awakening Fever / Mental Stamina Overload**, forcing the AI into throttled **Safe Mode (e.g. 10% capacity)** while the child recovers.
   - **Strict Observation Gate:** The protagonist cannot magically use all affinities at once. An affinity can ONLY be unlocked after the AI **observes that magic cast in person** (e.g. learning Base Elements by watching family members and town mages, while Hidden Elements remain 100% LOCKED at `0% Data` until witnessed in future arcs).
4. **Credible Family Lineage & Honest Past-Life Confession:**
   - Avoid making parents unrealistically gullible when a child suddenly displays advanced engineering knowledge. Ground the parents with a credible background (e.g. a **fallen Artisan-Crafter family** limited by single affinities) and dedicate a full multi-batch emotional sequence where the protagonist **honestly confesses their awakened past-life memories**, earning the family's informed partnership.
5. **Strict Tech-Tree Chronology (Foundations Before Machinery & Electricity):**
   - Millennia-old medieval problems cannot be solved overnight with cars, wind turbines, or electricity in Episode 1.
   - Early arcs MUST focus strictly on foundational survival, hygiene, and stone masonry (charcoal-sand-pebble water filtration, gravity-fed stone aqueducts, wood-ash lye & herbal soap saponification, enclosed draft chimneys), reserving mechanical gears, bearings, turbines, and electricity for later arcs (Arcs III–IV).
